# Code of Conduct

Device Sentinel is built by one person and a few testers, in their spare time, for people who want to know when a device in their house stops working. This page says how we treat each other in its issues, discussions, pull requests and wiki, and anywhere someone speaks for the project.

## What We Ask

- **Be kind and patient.** Many people here are new to Home Assistant. A question that seems obvious to you is a real question to them.
- **Assume good faith.** A short or blunt message is usually a busy person, not a rude one.
- **Stay on the subject.** Keep each issue or discussion to Device Sentinel and the problem at hand.
- **Disagree about the work, not the person.** Criticism of a design, a rule or a release is welcome. Say what you see and why.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not post someone else's house files, names, addresses or diagnostics without their permission. Check your own before you attach them.

## What Is Not Accepted

- Insults, harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Comments about a person's race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age, nationality or appearance.
- Spam, advertising, or repeated off-topic posts.
- Publishing anyone's private information.

## Reporting, and What Happens

If something here makes you uncomfortable, open the three-dot menu on the comment and choose "Report content", then "Report to repository admins". The report reaches the maintainer privately.

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, lock a thread, or block an account that breaks these rules. Most of the time a short reminder is enough.
